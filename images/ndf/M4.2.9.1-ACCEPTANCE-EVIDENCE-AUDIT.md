## 1. Purpose and method

This is a read-only assessment of the implementation and evidence already present at the audit baseline. It does not change implementation behavior, retroactively promote a deferred result to a pass, or authorize production release/deployment.

The review inspected the approved M4.2 plan and tracker, the M4.2.4–M4.2.8 package records, the M4.2.6.7 and M4.2.7 final acceptance records, the candidate producer/consumer contract, the cross-workflow acceptance record, and the relevant NDF workflow/script/schema/test inventory.

Status terminology:

- **Satisfied (scoped):** the required behavior has evidence for the stated product/platform or repository-local contract. The scope limitation remains part of the result.
- **Partial:** some local behavior is verified, but a required part of the criterion or its evidence is not yet established.
- **Deferred (external):** the evidence depends on DT Master, a real producer identity, or a separately authorized operational action. This is neither passed nor failed.
- **Documentation reconciliation:** implementation evidence exists, but a stale or contradictory record prevents an unambiguous parent acceptance claim.

## 3. Work-package reconciliation findings

### 3.1 M4.2.4 — Immutable Image Construction

The tracker records M4.2.4 as **In Progress**, with M4.2.4.3 and .4 accepted for Dalali v16 AMD64. Run #95 is real Buildx evidence: it generated fresh lock-v2 data, used digest-pinned builder/runtime inputs, captured the 14-app/Frappe post-Bench inventory, extracted the inventory from the runtime image, and independently matched the lock/inventory/image digest identities in the uploaded artifact.

The package record still contains an unchecked initial acceptance checklist and unresolved historical discussion text. In particular, it describes the M4.2.5 verifier boundary, evidence transport and publication/retention questions as open even though M4.2.5 and M4.2.6 subsequently implemented and accepted their respective responsibilities. Those historical statements must not be mistaken for current package status.

Open reconciliation items:

1. Inspect the current Containerfile, prepare/build scripts and product workflows against M4.2.4 §4.1–§4.5.
2. Confirm the accepted product build matrix and identify exactly which product/platform combinations have real Buildx evidence.
3. Reconcile the M4.2.4 checklist with run #95, subsequent run #172, and the separately accepted M4.2.5 verifier/evidence handoff.
4. Preserve known provenance limitations: captured dependency-file candidates do not prove installer consumption; available uv/pip versions do not prove which Bench installer branch was selected. Do not silently elevate these observations into proof.
5. Decide whether these limitations block any approved M4.2 acceptance criterion or are bounded provenance limitations to carry forward explicitly.
6. Update the M4.2.4 package record and tracker only after the code/workflow and evidence review is complete.

No change to the upstream Bench acquisition or native package-manager ownership model is proposed.
