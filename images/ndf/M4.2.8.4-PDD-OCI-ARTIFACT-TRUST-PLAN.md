# M4.2.8.4 — PDD OCI Artifact Signing and Independent Trust Verification

**Repository:** `NdfTechLabs/frappe_docker`
**Status:** M4.2.8.4.1–.4.4.5 accepted; .4.5.1 contract accepted; .4.5.2 accepted; .4.5.3 orchestration contract proposed, pending acceptance
**Date:** 16 November 2026
**Depends on:** M4.2.8.1 trust contract, M4.2.8.2 trust data/validation foundation, M4.2.8.3 product-image signing and live Cosign interoperability

## 1. Objective

Extend NDF artifact trust to Product Deployment Descriptor (PDD) OCI artifacts. NDF must sign the exact immutable PDD OCI digest, attach a PDD-to-image binding attestation whose subject is that PDD digest, independently verify the signature and attestation, and require the consumer retrieval path to verify trust before reporting a PDD as verified.

The existing PDD, image digest, canonical content hash, evidence-bundle and publication-receipt contracts remain authoritative. Signing and attestation are additional authenticated evidence; they do not replace existing PDD validation or evidence binding.

## 3. Required trust contract

### 3.1 PDD signature subject

- Sign the immutable PDD OCI reference `repository@sha256:<digest>`, never a mutable locator tag.
- The signed subject digest must equal the manifest digest recorded in the publication receipt.
- The signature must use GitHub Actions keyless signing through the pinned Cosign v3.1.4 toolchain.
- The exact product-PDD workflow identity is `https://github.com/NdfTechLabs/frappe_docker/.github/workflows/ndf-publish-product-deployment-descriptor.yml@refs/heads/main`; the OIDC issuer is `https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com`.
- Identity, issuer, event, repository, ref and source-SHA checks remain exact. The policy remains provisional until live evidence is separately reviewed.

### 3.2 PDD-to-image binding attestation

Generate an in-toto Statement v1 using predicate type:

`https://ndftechlabs.com/attestations/pdd-image-binding/v1`

The statement subject must be the exact PDD OCI repository and digest. The predicate must bind, without inferred or fabricated values:

- PDD descriptor ID and canonical content SHA-256;
- PDD OCI repository and manifest digest;
- subject image repository, immutable digest and platform;
- exact lock-v2 SHA-256;
- source image provenance predicate type and canonical predicate SHA-256 (`predicate_sha256`);
- originating NDF workflow repository, path, ref, run ID, attempt and source commit.

The generator must derive values from the validated PDD, publication record, independently verified source-image provenance and successful PDD/image binding report. It must reject disagreement between any source. `source_provenance.predicate_sha256` is SHA-256 over the UTF-8 bytes of the verified SLSA predicate JSON object serialized with NDF-JSON-CANONICAL-V1 (sorted keys, compact separators, `ensure_ascii=False`, one trailing LF). It does not hash the Statement wrapper, DSSE envelope, Sigstore bundle or Rekor entry. The previous ambiguous field name `attestation_sha256` is rejected by the updated schema.

### 3.3 Verification and consumer behavior

The publication trust stage must independently verify:

1. The PDD OCI signature against the exact immutable PDD digest.
2. The PDD-image-binding attestation against the same PDD digest.
3. Exact certificate identity, issuer, workflow trigger, repository, ref and source SHA.
4. Normal Sigstore trusted-root and Rekor inclusion verification.
5. Full Statement v1 schema and exact PDD subject binding.
6. Exact predicate equality against the locally regenerated expected binding predicate.
7. PDD descriptor ID/content hash, image repository/digest/platform and lock hash agreement with the independently verified PDD and source evidence.

The fetch/consumer path must independently verify the PDD signature and binding attestation and retrieve/verify the source-image SLSA attestation by the immutable image digest. It computes `predicate_sha256` from the verified predicate and compares it to the binding claim before it reports `verified`. The consumer must use a self-contained verification mode; it must not trust a caller-supplied expected predicate or a local build artifact. A valid publication receipt alone is not proof of cryptographic trust. Missing, invalid, ambiguous, mismatched or unavailable trust evidence must fail closed.

## 4. Workflow and permission boundaries

- Keep PDD generation and existing binding verification as prerequisites.
- Keep OCI publication and immutable digest resolution in the existing publication workflow.
- Add a separate signing job/stage with job-scoped `id-token: write` and only the GHCR permissions required to attach the signature/attestation.
- Do not grant OIDC token permission to the build, PDD generation, binding-verification or consumer-verification jobs unless the job itself performs keyless signing (the consumer must not sign).
- Consumer verification is read-only: `contents: read`, `packages: read`, and minimum required `actions: read`; no OIDC token or package write permission.
- Pass only immutable PDD digest references and validated evidence artifact identities between jobs.
- Do not pass OIDC tokens, registry credentials or secrets through outputs or artifacts.
- Do not grant `contents: write`, `actions: write`, repository administration or broader permissions.
- Do not weaken product image signing, source provenance, PDD evidence binding or candidate authorization gates.
- Do not implement candidate image/PDD signing here.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

M4.2.8.4 is complete only when:

1. The PDD OCI digest is immutable, independently resolved and identical across publication receipt, signature subject and binding attestation subject.
2. The PDD signature verifies cryptographically under the exact product-PDD identity and issuer constraints.
3. The PDD-image-binding attestation verifies under the same exact identity and issuer constraints, with normal Rekor verification.
4. The complete Statement v1 and predicate validate against the existing schema.
5. The attested predicate exactly matches independently regenerated values from the validated PDD and source evidence.
6. Descriptor ID/content hash, image digest/repository/platform, lock hash and source provenance digest agree across all relevant records.
7. The consumer fetch path verifies signature, attestation and all existing evidence bindings before returning success.
8. Missing, altered, mismatched, malformed, mutable or unverifiable evidence fails closed.
9. Signing permissions are isolated to the signing job; consumer verification is read-only.
10. A real staging-only PDD publish/sign/verify/fetch round trip is captured; no production promotion or candidate trust is introduced.
11. The normalized trust result remains rejected while the signer identity policy is provisional. Policy confirmation is a separate reviewed action.

## 7. Explicitly out of scope

- Changing `product-pdd-publication` identity policy status to confirmed.
- Trust-gated production promotion or release workflow changes.
- Candidate image/PDD signing and reusable workflow identity observation.
- DT Master consumer implementation.
- DT Master-supplied candidate artifact signing.
- Changes to the existing product-image provenance contract.
- Weakening or bypassing source provenance, PDD evidence binding, Rekor verification, signer identity or authorization checks.
