## 1. Objective

Demonstrate that the merged product-image publication workflow performs the required build, immutable-digest signing and independent trust verification, permits staging-only execution under the provisional identity policy without granting production trust, and blocks official promotion when that policy remains provisional.

This package records two live workflow-dispatch runs against the same merged `main` commit. The positive staging run and the deliberately rejected official-promotion run are complementary acceptance cases.

## 6. Disposition

**M4.2.8.3.6 is accepted and closed. M4.2.8.3 is complete and accepted for its product-image signing, provenance-attestation, independent-verification and promotion-gate scope.**

The nonzero conclusion of run #8 is the expected result of the negative security test. It is acceptance evidence that the promotion gate fails closed; it is not an implementation defect and must not be changed to a green outcome by weakening the policy.

The two runs demonstrate the merged product-image publication path on `main`. They do not establish successful official promotion, production trust, deployment authorization, the separate product-release workflow's live acceptance, DT Master candidate-origin integration, or the end-to-end M4.2.8.6 scope.

## 7. Security and authorization boundary

- Signer identity policy remains `provisional_until_live_certificate_observation`.
- Trust decisions remain rejected with `IDENTITY_POLICY_PROVISIONAL`.
- Deployment authorization remains `not_granted`.
- No official product tag was promoted by either run.
- NDF signs only the NDF-produced product image; it does not sign or re-attest DT Master-produced candidate artifacts.
- Do not interpret successful cryptographic verification as policy confirmation or production eligibility.
